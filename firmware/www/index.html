Target: a swing of at least 500 mV, signal between 300 and 2500 mV. Reset before trying each new distance or resistor value. Not enough swing: vary the distance, change the emitter resistor, tilt the sensor by 10–15°, add a black divider between LED and phototransistor.

Edge detection and volume

Thresholds

Two, not one: without hysteresis the signal bounces at the transition and every edge counts several times. Rule of thumb: 30 % and 70 % of the swing -- "Derive thresholds from swing" above does exactly that.

Edge filter

A crossing counts only after lasting the dwell time; shorter ones show up as rejected above. Edges closer than the minimum gap wait: at full flow they are 0.58 s apart. If real edges get rejected, a threshold sits too close to a sector's level.

Volume per edge

From the bucket method: draw 10 L, see what was counted, correct the factor. Expect 250, 500 or 1000 ml; an odd value usually means spurious pulses.

Taking the adapter off

Pause first, so handling the adapter cannot count phantom litres. On resume the device first reads which sector the sensor sees and only then counts again. Unplugging or light falling in also stops counting by itself after half a second, and it resumes 2 s after the signal is steady again.

Match the mechanical register

Absolute, not a correction: enter the reading as shown. The red digits are the decimals: 00044 and 2670 make 44.2670.

Broker

Server certificate

Public authorities fit a broker with a certificate from e.g. Let's Encrypt or a cloud broker; the host must then be the name in its certificate. A self-signed broker or one from your own CA needs its CA certificate. Without checking, the connection is encrypted but anybody could pose as the broker.

Off: the device connects anonymously, and stored credentials are deleted on Save.

Topics

Announces meter reading, flow and status, so Home Assistant creates the device by itself.

Radio

Takes effect on Apply, no restart. Keep an eye on the signal level top right while trying steps: every 3 dB less halves the transmit power. After a brownout the firmware steps it down on its own.

Time

A built-in server always stands behind the one entered here, so a wrong or vanished one costs nothing; left empty, only the built-in ones are used. The device counts in UTC; the timezone sets how times are shown here.

Device and access

The device name is also the mDNS hostname and the MQTT client ID. The Home Assistant identity, by contrast, is tied to the MAC, so renaming the device does not orphan any entities.

Status LED

In normal operation, count the flashes; they repeat every 10 seconds. While water flows, each detected edge adds a short extra flash.

1 flash, pauseOnline, all good
2 flashes, pauseWiFi configured but not connected
3 flashes, pauseOnline, but the sensor signal is implausible: sensor loose, IR LED dead or wiring wrong
Even fast blinking (5 Hz)Setup network open, waiting for WiFi credentials

Solid onBooting, or BOOT button held: release now to keep the WiFi settings

Firmware update

The new image first runs on probation: it has to pass its own self-test (30 s without a crash, measuring, on the network, web server answering) and this page confirms it once it is back. Otherwise the device falls back to the previous firmware within two minutes. Keep this page open and do not disconnect power during the update.
